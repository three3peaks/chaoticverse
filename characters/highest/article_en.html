Highest is a human from the real world who sees himself as a god and a higher being. Characters are toys, universes are canvases, the multiverse is his field of experiments. You can love him, hate him, condemn him, or forgive him. But he does not care about your feelings or thoughts. You are just another pawn in his game—even if you already know it.

Backstory

At first he tried to create a universe of his own, but nothing he made ever felt original or interesting. After that he decided to do a take on an existing universe, and people liked his reinterpretation. That is how he began to gain fame, fans, and admirers of his work. But all of it only fed his ego. The more people loved his work, the higher his self-regard grew, until it reached its absolute peak—where he came to see himself as a genius capable of “improving” any universe, popular or not. He would always find those who would admire him. That is how he named himself Highest. A higher being, subject to no one, capable of anything he desires.

Personality

Highest is extremely vain and selfish. He always considers himself the best and never doubts that he is right. He reacts very sharply to any criticism aimed at him—either dismissing it entirely or mocking it. And any praise only feeds his already excessive ego. Needless to say, the words of any inhabitant are empty noise to him—something he neither hears nor takes into account. In his attempts to create a “perfect” universe he is willing to ignore every boundary of morality and law, as long as he reaches that one ideal he alone can see. And even if he makes mistakes and knows they were his fault, he will still convince himself that this was how he planned it all along, refusing to admit his own shortsightedness.
The only ones he might consider equals are other hackers who also manipulate code and do whatever they want. Even then, Highest would never dare call them friends. He is more likely to see them as juniors who cannot grasp his genius. Only one person ever earned from him the right to be called a friend. And that same person betrayed him—finally shattering what little trust he still had in people.


Appearance

Highest looks human, but his body is only an avatar made of code and nothing else. There is not a single fragment of a real human in him. This is clearest in his black-and-red soul, which is literally part of his body and fills almost the entire space of his torso, its roots spreading through him.
His hair is bright purple, with a long braided ponytail. His eyes are black, with red irises. He wears a white T-shirt with white trousers, a dark purple belt, and a coat of the same color whose interior is a black-and-red mathematical grid. On his feet he wears red-burgundy slippers with buckles.
In moments of extreme aggression or wild euphoria, his face can distort: his mouth becomes inhuman, and predatory red pupils appear.

Abilities

Code Hacking

A standard ability for any hacker—but Highest has advanced so far in the art of hacking that breaking even heavily protected systems is trivial for him, as is seizing control over entire saves and timelines of any universe. Hacking a character’s memories and personality is not a challenge for him, just a warm-up.

Attack

Technically, as a hacker, Highest can use any game mechanic available to him, but among his favorite attacks are black-and-red spikes that can also hack code. They can appear anywhere—Highest only needs to be close enough to the target, or push his power to the limit for a wide-area attack.

Red Sheets

The red sheets are sets of data converted into convenient documents that he can summon whenever he has access to them. Some contain technical information about different universes, as well as his plans for future worlds. But their main use is that they can store any data—even a character. Highest often uses this, keeping his experiments in them in the form of amalgamates. Most of them he uses as his combat force and personal army.

Personal Storage

Much like Core!Frisk, who can use their eyes as a way to store things, Highest uses his soul as a convenient storage for all kinds of junk.

Additional Information

  • Highest created the Brokenshift.
  • The Player is Highest’s main enemy, as well as his former friend. Despite their hostility, Highest still holds warm feelings toward him, but tries to convince himself otherwise.
  • Despite his abilities, Highest still cannot enter the Omega Timeline. He has been trying for a long time, so far without success.
  • Highest cannot be fully destroyed. He can only be temporarily cut off from the multiverse. The only way to defeat him is to make him bored.
  • Highest chose this appearance, inspired by an anime character.
  • Highest can rightfully be considered a skilled specialist in programming and rewriting other people’s code.
  • He hates imitators.
